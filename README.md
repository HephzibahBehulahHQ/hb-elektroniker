## About HB Elektroniker

HB Elektroniker is the electrical and electronics engineering division of **Hephzibah Behulah**.

We develop digital engineering tools, simulation platforms, educational applications and software solutions for electrical engineering, electronics, industrial automation and technical design.

Our goal is to make engineering tools more accessible, practical, interactive and reliable for students, technicians, engineers and technology enthusiasts.

## Our Products

### 1. Electrical & Electronics Simulator

Interactive tools for designing, building, testing and understanding electrical and electronic circuits.

### 2. Virtual Oscilloscope

A digital instrument for waveform visualization, signal analysis and electronics education.

### 3. ElectCAD

An electrical computer-aided design platform for schematic creation, engineering drawings and electrical documentation.

### 4. PLC Programming

Tools for learning, designing and simulating Programmable Logic Controller systems and industrial automation.

### 5. Virtual Breadboard

A digital environment for circuit prototyping, component placement and electronics experimentation.

### 6. Electrical Engineering Tools

Engineering calculators, electrical utilities, measurement tools and technical reference resources.

*These are product areas and development initiatives. Availability and release status will be documented individually.*
